Turn spare devices into extra screens

One device shares its screen and sound. Other devices join by scanning the host's QR code, then show a copy of it or each take a slice of one big picture.

Both devices need internet briefly to connect. After that, the video and sound go directly between the devices. Best when they're on the same Wi‑Fi.

Host

Tap “Add a screen” and let the other device scan the QR code. When sharing, tick Share audio so joiners can hear your sound.
